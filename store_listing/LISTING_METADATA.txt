## Full Description (Max 4,000 Chars)
PDF Voice Reader turns any PDF, ebook, or document into an offline listening experience using natural on-device text-to-speech. Whether you are commuting, exercising, or resting your eyes, this versatile read aloud app transforms books and study materials into personal audiobooks with continuous background playback.

✦ TURN ANY DOCUMENT INTO AN AUDIOBOOK
Transform your digital library into spoken audio in seconds. PDF Voice Reader extracts clean text from textbooks, research papers, work manuals, lecture notes, and public-domain books. Stop staring at dense screens—listen comfortably while you walk, cook, exercise, or commute.

✦ SMART MULTILINGUAL NARRATORS & VOICE PREVIEW
Find the ideal voice for every book. Filter Android text-to-speech voices across world languages—including English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, and more. The app automatically detects document language on-device, suggests native narrators, and saves your preferred voice per book. Tap any voice to hear a live preview before listening.

✦ SYNCHRONIZED WORD TRACKING & TAP-TO-READ
Follow along with real-time visual tracking that highlights each sentence and word as it speaks. Strengthen comprehension and language learning. Tap any word or sentence on the page to immediately relocate speech playback to that exact spot.

✦ CONTINUOUS BACKGROUND PLAYBACK
Keep listening with your screen off or while using other apps. Android media notifications provide lock-screen controls, drawer buttons, and headset support. A smart exit prompt lets you keep audio playing in the background.

✦ READERA-STYLE PAGE SCRUBBER & DUAL VIEW
Switch reading modes and navigate long titles with ease:
• Ebook Text Reflow: Clean formatted text with custom font sizes, line spacing, and margins.
• Original PDF Mode: Preserves exact page layouts, diagrams, charts, and formulas.
• Page Scrubber: Horizontal chips with active reading indicator for instant page jumps.

✦ SPEED CONTROL & AUDIO TUNING
Match playback tempo to your needs. Fine-tune your speech rate from 0.75x up to 2.0x turbo speed for fast study review, and calibrate voice pitch for your ideal listening comfort.

✦ MULTI-FORMAT SUPPORT
Bring your entire reading shelf into one unified offline voice reader:
• PDF documents (.pdf)
• EPUB ebooks (.epub)
• Plain text notes (.txt)
• Markdown files (.md)

✦ AUTOMATIC RESUME MEMORY
Never lose your place. PDF Voice Reader automatically saves your exact reading position down to the page and sentence, so you can resume listening instantly with one tap.

✦ 100% OFFLINE PRIVACY & ZERO CLOUD STORAGE
Your documents and reading habits stay strictly private. All text parsing and speech synthesis run 100% locally on your device with zero cloud uploads, no registration, and zero trackers. Enjoy books and notes with complete peace of mind.

✦ CRISP LIGHT & SLEEK DARK THEMES
Read comfortably day or night. Switch between a crisp light theme for daylight reading and a dark theme designed to minimize eye fatigue during late-night listening.

✦ HOW IT WORKS IN 3 SIMPLE STEPS
1. Import Document: Select any PDF, EPUB, TXT, or Markdown file from your device.
2. Select Narrator & Speed: Preview voices in your language and adjust your reading rate.
3. Listen & Absorb: Listen with headphones in the background or follow along on screen.

✦ DESIGNED FOR FOCUSED READERS
• Students & Academics: Review syllabus, research papers, and lecture notes hands-free.
• Busy Professionals: Turn whitepapers and reports into productive commute audio.
• Commuters & Drivers: Listen safely to books and articles while on the go.
• Language Learners: Master pronunciation by hearing native speakers with word highlighting.
• Accessible Reading: Ideal for anyone experiencing vision fatigue, dyslexia, or eye strain.

Experience a distraction-free, intelligent way to absorb knowledge. Download PDF Voice Reader and enjoy your favorite documents read aloud anywhere, anytime offline.
